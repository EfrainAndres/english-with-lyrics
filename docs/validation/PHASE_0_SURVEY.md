# Phase 0 Survey

## Objective

Validate whether Spanish-speaking English learners want to learn with Sing Pronunce Repeat / English with Lyrics before building more product infrastructure.

The survey should help decide:

- Whether learning English with songs is attractive enough to join a waitlist.
- Which pain points matter most: listening, pronunciation, vocabulary, confidence, or speaking.
- Which song categories should shape the free ebook and the first 5-song MVP.
- Whether learners want a first group after the free guide.
- Whether there is early willingness to pay in COP.
- Which delivery format feels easiest before a full web product exists.

## Target Audience

Primary:

- Spanish speakers learning English at beginner or intermediate level.
- Learners who already watch English with Lyrics-style educational content.
- People who enjoy songs but struggle to understand what they hear.

Strong early adopter signals:

- They mention listening or pronunciation as a real frustration.
- They want short practice, not a long course.
- They are interested in the free 3-song guide.
- They would consider joining a first group or paying later for a structured version.

## Recommended Tools

Use one simple tool first. Do not build a custom survey flow yet.

- Tally: recommended for a fast, polished Spanish survey with conditional logic.
- Typeform: useful if the brand needs a more guided, conversational survey.
- Google Forms: easiest if speed, low cost, and spreadsheet export matter most.

## Suggested Survey Questions

Keep the survey to 8 to 10 questions. Mark only essential questions as required.

### 1. ¿Cuál es tu nivel de inglés hoy?

Options:

- Estoy empezando desde cero.
- Básico.
- Intermedio.
- Avanzado, pero quiero mejorar escucha o pronunciación.
- No estoy seguro/a.

Use this to segment content difficulty and avoid overloading beginners.

### 2. ¿Qué te cuesta más cuando escuchas inglés en canciones?

Options:

- Entender lo que dicen.
- Pronunciar sin trabarme.
- Recordar vocabulario.
- Sentir confianza para cantar o hablar.
- Entender frases que no se traducen literal.
- Otro.

Use this as the main pain-point signal.

### 3. ¿Qué tan interesante te parece aprender inglés con canciones?

Options:

- Muy interesante, lo haría.
- Interesante, quiero ver un ejemplo.
- Tal vez, depende de las canciones.
- No es mi forma favorita de aprender.

Strong validation starts when most responses are in the first two options.

### 4. ¿Qué tipo de canciones te gustaría practicar primero?

Options:

- Románticas.
- Rock / pop clásicos.
- Pop actual.
- Música para principiantes.
- Canciones lentas y fáciles de repetir.
- Canciones que yo pueda sugerir.

Use this to prioritize the ebook paths and shortlist the first MVP songs.

### 5. ¿Te gustaría recibir una guía gratis con 3 canciones para practicar?

Options:

- Sí, la quiero.
- Sí, si es corta y fácil.
- Tal vez.
- No por ahora.

This validates the Phase 0.5 ebook lead magnet.

### 6. ¿Qué formato preferirías para empezar?

Options:

- PDF descargable.
- Videos cortos.
- Web con micro-lecciones.
- WhatsApp o email con ejercicios.
- App más adelante.

Use this to avoid building a full app before the audience asks for it.

### 7. ¿Qué debería incluir una buena lección con canciones?

Options:

- Pronunciación escrita como suena.
- Traducción natural al español.
- Explicación de frases reales.
- Ejercicios o quizzes cortos.
- Audio o video oficial para escuchar.
- Recomendaciones de práctica.

Allow multiple selections. Use this to rank lesson features.

### 8. Si abrimos un primer grupo con 5 canciones y 25 micro-lecciones, ¿te interesaría entrar?

Options:

- Sí, quiero recibir información.
- Tal vez, si el precio es accesible.
- Tal vez, si me gustan las canciones.
- No por ahora.

This validates first-group interest before payments.

### 9. Si más adelante fuera pago, ¿qué rango te parecería razonable?

Options:

- Menos de 30.000 COP.
- 30.000 a 50.000 COP.
- 50.000 a 80.000 COP.
- 80.000 a 120.000 COP.
- Más de 120.000 COP si el contenido es muy bueno.
- Prefiero contenido gratis.

Use this only as price sensitivity, not as final pricing proof.

### 10. ¿Quieres que te avisemos cuando esté lista la guía o el primer grupo?

Fields:

- Nombre.
- Email.
- País.
- Opcional: canción o artista que te gustaría practicar.

Include a consent checkbox:

- Acepto recibir novedades de Sing Pronunce Repeat / English with Lyrics sobre la guía gratis, encuesta y primer grupo.

## How To Interpret Responses

Review responses weekly while Phase 0 traffic is active.

Segment by:

- English level.
- Main pain point.
- Song category preference.
- Ebook interest.
- First group interest.
- Price range.
- Preferred format.

Prioritize learners who:

- Select listening, pronunciation, confidence, or phrases that do not translate literally.
- Want the free guide.
- Choose web, PDF, email, or video as acceptable first formats.
- Say yes or maybe to the first group.
- Leave a song request or specific learning frustration.

## Success Criteria

Strong validation:

- 25% or higher survey completion rate from landing visitors who start the survey.
- 40% or higher of survey respondents want the free guide.
- 20% or higher express clear first-group interest.
- At least 10 useful song or pain-point comments.
- A repeatable top pain point emerges.

Medium validation:

- Learners want the free guide but hesitate on the first group.
- The audience likes the method but needs clearer examples.
- Price answers cluster below the expected founder offer.

Weak validation:

- Most respondents only want full lyrics or translations.
- People do not understand the method.
- Interest depends entirely on specific songs.
- Few respondents want follow-up.

## Red Flags / When To Iterate

Iterate before building if:

- Users ask for complete lyrics, full translations, or a karaoke replacement.
- The phrase "Escríbelo como suena" is confusing without examples.
- Most people choose "Tal vez" across ebook and first-group questions.
- Learners want only free content and no structured support.
- The top requested format is a mobile app before demand is proven.
- Price ranges are too low for the planned scope.

## Data And Privacy Notes

Collect only what is needed for validation.

Do collect:

- Name.
- Email.
- Country.
- English level.
- Learning pain point.
- Song preferences.
- Consent for follow-up.

Do not collect:

- Government ID numbers.
- Payment data.
- Passwords.
- Home address.
- Sensitive personal information unrelated to learning.

Store responses in one controlled account, export them only when needed, and remove test or duplicate data before analysis.
