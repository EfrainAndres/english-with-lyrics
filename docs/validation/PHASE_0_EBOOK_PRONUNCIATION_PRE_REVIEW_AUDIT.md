## 1. Scope

This internal audit triages pronunciation risks in the approved nine-practice ebook material before external qualified review. It compares the current normalized bridges and learner notes with the intended learning baseline:

```text
Primary learning target: neutral General American English
Learner audience: Latin American Spanish speakers
Singer-specific pronunciation: documented separately
Official audio: final listening reference
Bridge purpose: practical first approximation, not exact phonetics
```

The audit distinguishes neutral spoken pronunciation, connected phrase pronunciation, singer/accent/style differences, Spanish-friendly approximation, and temporary support that should later be reduced. It does not change an approved expression, bridge, learner note, standard, or learner-facing page.

## 2. Limitations

```text
AI-assisted pronunciation pre-review:
May identify risks, inconsistencies and candidate corrections.

Qualified pronunciation review:
Must be completed or confirmed by a person with relevant English
pronunciation, phonetics or ELT experience, preferably teaching
Spanish-speaking learners.

AI pre-review does not complete the qualified-review gate.
```

Risk means the risk of sending a Latin American Spanish learner toward a materially misleading sound. It does not mean general exercise difficulty.
