# English with Lyrics Content Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines how English with Lyrics should create educational song-based content while keeping Tania's method clear, useful, and legally cautious.

## Core Teaching Format

Each lesson should be built around a short educational fragment, not a full song.

Recommended lesson structure:

- Song context.
- Short fragment.
- Escríbelo como suena pronunciation guide.
- Natural translation.
- Meaning and usage explanation.
- Listening tip.
- Short quiz.
- Practice prompt.

## Escríbelo Como Suena Rules

Escríbelo como suena is Tania's Spanish-friendly pronunciation method. It should help Spanish speakers approximate English sounds without IPA symbols.

Rules:

- Use simple Spanish-friendly spelling.
- Avoid IPA.
- Make the guide easy to read aloud.
- Prioritize useful approximation over perfect phonetic precision.
- Explain when a sound does not exist naturally in Spanish.
- Use hyphens, spacing, or emphasis only when they improve readability.
- Keep Tania's teaching voice human, direct, and encouraging.
- Do not present the guide as the only correct pronunciation.
- Encourage learners to listen to the official audio.

## Translation Rules

- Use natural Spanish translation.
- Explain meaning, tone, and context when literal translation is awkward.
- Avoid over-translating idioms without explanation.
- Keep translations tied to the short educational fragment.
- Do not publish full translated lyrics.

## Song Fragment Rules

- Use only short fragments needed for teaching.
- Choose fragments that demonstrate pronunciation, meaning, grammar, or listening patterns.
- Do not compile fragments in a way that reconstructs most or all of a song.
- Do not create pages that function as a lyrics database.
- Do not use commercial song lyrics as decorative page content.

## YouTube Usage

- Embed official YouTube videos when needed.
- Prefer official artist, label, or licensed YouTube sources.
- Do not download or rehost commercial music videos or audio.
- Use the embed to support listening practice, not to replace official platforms.

## Tone

English with Lyrics should feel:

- Warm.
- Practical.
- Friendly to beginners.
- Creator-led.
- Clear and motivating.
- Respectful of learner effort.

Avoid:

- Overly academic explanations.
- Claims of instant fluency.
- Mocking pronunciation mistakes.
- Presenting pronunciation approximations as exact science.

## Lesson Quality Checklist

Before publishing a lesson, confirm:

- The fragment is short and educational.
- The pronunciation guide is original.
- The translation is natural and limited.
- The explanation adds teaching value.
- The lesson points learners back to official audio.
- The content cannot replace full lyrics.
- The quiz reinforces the learning goal.
