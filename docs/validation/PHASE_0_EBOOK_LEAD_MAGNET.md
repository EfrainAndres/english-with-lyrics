# Phase 0 Ebook Lead Magnet

## Objective

Use a free 3-song guide to validate whether learners want more structured song-based English practice before building the paid MVP.

The ebook should:

- Give a quick win with short educational fragments.
- Demonstrate the Escríbelo como suena method.
- Invite readers to the waitlist, survey, and first group interest list.
- Help identify which song categories and learning formats create the strongest demand.

## Recommended Ebook Title

**Aprende inglés con canciones: 3 prácticas para escuchar, pronunciar y entender**

Subtitle:

**Una guía de Sing Pronounce Repeat / English with Lyrics con el método Escríbelo como suena.**

## Three Ebook Paths

Create one small guide with three paths, or three lightweight variants if segmentation becomes useful.

### 1. Románticas

Best for:

- Learners who want slower, emotional phrases.
- Beginners who enjoy clear repetition.
- People who connect with meaning and tone.

Teaching angle:

- Emotion, natural translation, connected words, and pronunciation confidence.

### 2. Rock / Pop Clásicos

Best for:

- Learners motivated by familiar songs.
- Adults who want recognizable references.
- People who want useful everyday phrases.

Teaching angle:

- High-frequency expressions, rhythm, reductions, and words that change when sung.

### 3. Principiantes

Best for:

- Learners who feel intimidated by English songs.
- People who need short, slow, clear practice.
- Students who want confidence before harder music.

Teaching angle:

- Very short fragments, simple pronunciation, natural meaning, and one practice action per page.

## Suggested Ebook Structure

Keep the ebook short enough to finish in one sitting.

Recommended sections:

1. Cover.
2. What this guide helps you practice.
3. How Escríbelo como suena works.
4. Legal-safe content note.
5. Song practice 1.
6. Song practice 2.
7. Song practice 3.
8. Review page: what you practiced.
9. CTA: join the waitlist and answer the survey.
10. CTA: first group interest.

## What Each Song Section Should Include

Each song section should teach one to three short educational fragments only.

Include:

- Song title and artist reference.
- Link or instruction to listen on an official platform.
- One short educational phrase at a time.
- Original Escríbelo como suena pronunciation guide.
- Natural Spanish translation.
- Short meaning or usage note.
- Listening tip.
- Repeat prompt.
- One tiny quiz or reflection question.
- CTA to request more lessons like that one.

Example format:

- Inglés: short educational phrase.
- Como suena: Spanish-friendly original pronunciation guide.
- Español natural: meaning in Spanish.
- Tip: what to listen for.
- Practica: repeat twice with the official audio.

## Legal-Safe Content Rules For The Ebook

Required:

- Use only short educational fragments.
- Add original teaching value around every fragment.
- Use original pronunciation guidance.
- Use natural translation only for the short fragment being taught.
- Point learners to official audio or video sources.
- Include a note that the guide is educational and does not replace official lyrics, audio, or video platforms.

Avoid:

- Full lyrics.
- Full translated lyrics.
- Long excerpts.
- Reconstructing most of a song through many fragments.
- Decorative lyrics.
- Downloading, embedding, or distributing commercial audio.
- Promising exact phonetic transcription.

Suggested legal-safe note:

Esta guía enseña con fragmentos educativos cortos, pronunciación original, traducción natural y explicaciones transformativas. No publica letras completas ni reemplaza plataformas oficiales de música, video o lyrics.

## CTA From Ebook To Waitlist / First Group

Primary CTA:

**Quiero más canciones para practicar**

Supporting copy:

Únete a la lista de espera para recibir nuevas prácticas, responder la encuesta de canciones y enterarte si abrimos un primer grupo pequeño.

Survey CTA:

**Ayudar a elegir las próximas canciones**

First group CTA:

**Quiero información del primer grupo**

Supporting copy:

El primer grupo todavía no tiene pagos activos. Primero vamos a validar canciones, formato, precio en COP y condiciones.

## Metrics To Track

Track these in a simple spreadsheet until there is enough traffic for analytics tooling:

- Landing visits by traffic source.
- Ebook CTA clicks.
- Waitlist signups.
- Survey completions.
- Selected ebook path.
- Song category preference.
- Song requests.
- First group interest.
- Preferred format.
- Preferred COP price range.
- Replies or comments about the guide.
- Clicks from ebook to survey or first group interest.

Decision signals:

- Strong: people download the guide, answer the survey, and ask for more songs.
- Medium: people download the guide but do not show first-group interest.
- Weak: people only ask for full lyrics or do not complete the survey.
