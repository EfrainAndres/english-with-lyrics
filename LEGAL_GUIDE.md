# English with Lyrics Legal Guide

## Purpose

This guide sets working rules for legally cautious product and content decisions. It is not legal advice. Before scaling paid content, consult a qualified attorney familiar with copyright, education, and digital products in the relevant markets.

## Core Legal Position

Sing Pronounce Repeat / English with Lyrics should be a transformative educational product. It should teach pronunciation, listening, meaning, and English learning skills through short song fragments, original commentary, and original Escríbelo como suena guidance.

The platform must not be designed or presented as a lyrics replacement.

## Copyright Rules

- Do not publish full lyrics from commercial songs.
- Do not publish full translated lyrics from commercial songs.
- Do not create lesson pages that reconstruct a full song through many fragments.
- Do not store or distribute commercial audio files.
- Do not store or distribute commercial music videos.
- Use only short fragments necessary for educational commentary.
- Add original teaching value around every fragment.
- Embed official YouTube videos when needed.
- Prefer links and embeds from official or licensed sources.

## Content Transformation Requirements

Every song-based lesson should include original educational value, such as:

- Original Escríbelo como suena pronunciation guidance.
- Natural translation of the short fragment.
- Listening explanation.
- Vocabulary or grammar explanation.
- Cultural or usage context.
- Quiz questions.
- Practice prompts.

Fragments should support teaching. They should not be the main product by themselves.

## Product Positioning Rules

Allowed positioning:

- Learn English through songs.
- Practice pronunciation with the Escríbelo como suena method.
- Understand short song fragments with natural Spanish explanations.
- Improve listening confidence through micro-lessons.

Avoid positioning:

- Full lyrics library.
- Song translation database.
- Free lyrics replacement.
- Downloadable lyrics collection.
- Karaoke or sing-along replacement for licensed products.

## User-Generated Content

If user-generated content is added in the future:

- Do not allow users to upload full lyrics.
- Do not allow users to upload copyrighted audio or video.
- Add moderation and takedown procedures.
- Add terms that prohibit copyright infringement.

## Payment And Access

Before real COP payments:

- Define refund terms.
- Define founder offer delivery scope.
- Define access duration.
- Define support expectations.
- Add clear terms and privacy policy.

## Required Review Before Scaling

Get legal review before:

- Launching paid MVP broadly.
- Publishing many lessons based on commercial songs.
- Adding user uploads.
- Running partner programs.
- Creating a mobile app with app store distribution.
- Using AI features trained on or generating copyrighted song content.
