## 4. Reviewer qualification criteria

The preferred reviewer should have relevant experience in at least one of these categories:

- Teaching English pronunciation to Spanish speakers.
- English-language phonetics or phonology.
- TESOL, TEFL or ELT pronunciation instruction.
- Accent and intelligibility coaching for Latin American learners.

No specific qualification is claimed to be legally required. The record should identify the reviewer role and relevant experience category without unnecessary personal information.

## 9. Gate criteria

The pronunciation gate may pass only when:

- A qualified reviewer role and review date are recorded.
- Target accent is confirmed.
- Every global convention has a verdict.
- All nine practices have a reviewer verdict.
- Every `CHANGE REQUIRED` item has an approved replacement.
- Every `PASS WITH CAVEAT` verdict has a documented caveat.
- Every caveat has a recorded disposition.
- Every learner-facing caveat has project-owner acceptance.
- No unresolved caveat remains.
- Standard and improved content are synchronized.
- No bridge remains `DRAFT — QUALIFIED REVIEW PENDING`.
- A post-change consistency check passes.
- No approved English expression is changed.
- Owner accepts any learner-facing pronunciation changes.
