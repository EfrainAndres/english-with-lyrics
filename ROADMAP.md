# English with Lyrics Roadmap

## Strategy

Build the smallest web-first path that proves people want to learn English with the Sing Pronounce Repeat song-based method, then expand only after evidence supports it.

## Phase 0: Validation

Goal: Confirm demand before building the platform.

Deliverables:

- Landing page.
- Survey.
- Waitlist.
- Founder offer.
- Clear positioning around Sing Pronounce Repeat, English with Lyrics, and Escríbelo como suena.
- Manual follow-up process for interested learners.

Key questions:

- Do learners understand the offer?
- Will they join the waitlist?
- Will they answer a survey?
- Will they pay or reserve access for a founder version?

Not included:

- Full app.
- Auth.
- Payments integration.
- Course dashboard.

## Phase 0.5: Free Ebook Lead Magnet

Goal: Grow the audience and validate educational content format.

Deliverables:

- Free ebook with 3 songs.
- Short educational fragments only.
- Original Escríbelo como suena pronunciation guidance.
- Natural translation and explanations.
- Email capture flow.
- Follow-up sequence that invites users to the founder offer.

Key questions:

- Do learners download the ebook?
- Do they find the method useful?
- Do they want more songs after the ebook?

## Phase 1: MVP

Goal: Sell and deliver the first paid web product.

Deliverables:

- 5 songs converted into 25 micro-lessons.
- Drip content.
- Short quizzes.
- Simple progress tracking.
- Real COP payments.
- Basic access management.
- Embedded official YouTube videos when needed.

Key questions:

- Will users pay in COP?
- Will users complete lessons?
- Which songs and lesson formats create the most engagement?
- Does the method improve confidence and comprehension?

## Phase 2: SEO And Content Expansion

Goal: Turn validated content into discoverable web pages.

Potential deliverables:

- SEO-friendly song lesson previews.
- Topic pages for pronunciation patterns.
- More songs.
- Internal linking between lessons.
- Public educational snippets that do not replace lyrics.

## Phase 3: Community And Social Impact

Goal: Expand access and trust.

Potential deliverables:

- Social scholarships.
- Partnerships with schools, teachers, or community groups.
- Cohort experiments.
- Testimonials and learner stories.

## Phase 4: Advanced Product

Goal: Add technology only after core learning demand is proven.

Potential deliverables:

- Mobile app.
- AI-assisted practice.
- Personalized recommendations.
- Richer progress analytics.
- Teacher or partner dashboards.

## Roadmap Guardrails

- Do not build the app before Phase 0 validation assets are ready.
- Do not add payments before the founder offer is clear.
- Do not add AI before there is enough real lesson content and learner behavior.
- Do not create full-lyrics pages.
- Keep the web app as the first product surface.
