# Phase 0 Ebook Qualified Pronunciation Review Packet

## 1. Purpose

This packet prepares the approved nine-practice ebook pronunciation material for external review. It records reviewer evidence and decisions without changing the approved English expressions, current normalized bridges, learner notes, or improved 32-page learner copy.

```text
AI-assisted pronunciation pre-review:
May identify risks, inconsistencies and candidate corrections.

Qualified pronunciation review:
Must be completed or confirmed by a person with relevant English
pronunciation, phonetics or ELT experience, preferably teaching
Spanish-speaking learners.

AI pre-review does not complete the qualified-review gate.
```

## 3. Reviewer profile

The preferred reviewer has relevant experience in at least one of these categories:

- Teaching English pronunciation to Spanish speakers.
- English-language phonetics or phonology.
- TESOL, TEFL or ELT pronunciation instruction.
- Accent and intelligibility coaching for Latin American learners.

No particular qualification is represented as a legal requirement. The reviewer should be able to assess whether a Spanish-friendly bridge is practical without materially misleading the target learner.

## 4. Target-accent decision

Review against this proposed baseline and record whether it is accepted:

```text
Primary learning target: neutral General American English
Learner audience: Latin American Spanish speakers
Singer-specific pronunciation: documented separately
Official audio: final listening reference
Bridge purpose: practical first approximation, not exact phonetics
Target accent confirmed by qualified reviewer: PENDING
```

The review must distinguish:

1. Neutral spoken pronunciation.
2. Connected phrase pronunciation.
3. Singer/accent/style differences.
4. Spanish-friendly approximation.
5. Temporary support that should later be reduced.

British and American targets must not be mixed silently. Any departure from the General American baseline must be identified and justified.

## 5. Review method

For each practice, the reviewer should:

1. Read only the approved short expression, current bridge, and current learner note in its isolated row.
2. Check neutral spoken pronunciation using at least one reputable English pronunciation dictionary or equivalent reference.
3. Use a second reputable reference for any disputed or high-risk sound.
4. Compare connected phrase pronunciation with the neutral target.
5. Use official song audio only to evaluate singer-specific realization.
6. Assess the bridge for a Latin American Spanish learner and identify materially misleading cues.
7. Record a verdict, approved replacement when required, concise evidence, and review date.

Evidence fields may contain source names and review dates. Do not add raw song URLs. Do not add adjacent song lines, reconstruct a verse or chorus, or introduce any new song expression.

Allowed verdicts:

```text
PASS
PASS WITH CAVEAT
CHANGE REQUIRED
NOT REVIEWED
```

### PASS WITH CAVEAT disposition

PASS WITH CAVEAT requires a documented caveat and disposition.

Allowed dispositions:

- Documentation only.
- Learner-note change required.
- Bridge change required.
- No action required, with reviewer justification.

Any caveat affecting learner-facing material requires project-owner acceptance before publication. No unresolved caveat may remain when the pronunciation gate passes.

## 11. Change-application authorization

No source change is authorized by packet preparation or by the AI-assisted pre-review. If the qualified reviewer marks an item `CHANGE REQUIRED`, the reviewer must provide an approved bridge and/or approved learner note in the corresponding empty field. Learner-facing changes may be applied only after the review is complete, evidence is recorded, and the owner accepts the changes.

After authorized changes, the pronunciation standard and improved content must be synchronized and pass a post-change consistency check. Approved English expressions must remain unchanged.
